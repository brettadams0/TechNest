# Shopping at TechNest

A short guide for customers. TechNest is a demo store: orders are real records you can track,
but nothing is charged and nothing ships.

## Finding products

- **Search** from the bar at the top of every page. It matches product names, descriptions and
  categories.
- **Browse** the catalog by category from the sidebar (a row of chips on a phone).
- **Sort** by featured, price, newest or name, and tick **In stock only** to hide sold-out items.

Stock labels are live: *Only 3 left* means exactly three, and a sold-out product cannot be added to a
cart.

## Choosing options

Most products have option groups such as colour, storage or warranty. Pick one value from each
group; any extra cost is shown next to the choice, and the price at the top updates as you choose.

## Your cart

The cart keeps your choices while you browse, even before you sign in. Every time you open it, it is
checked against the current catalog. If something changed — an item was withdrawn, an option
removed, or stock fell below your quantity — the cart fixes itself and tells you what happened, so
you are never charged for something different from what you reviewed.

Change a quantity and it saves immediately (with JavaScript off, use **Update**). Setting it to 0, or
the bin icon, removes the line.

## Checking out

You need an account to check out; if you are not signed in you will be sent to sign in and brought
straight back. Enter a Canadian shipping address — the postal code is checked and formatted for you
(`n9b3p4` becomes `N9B 3P4`).

The summary shows every part of the total before you commit:

- **Shipping** is free on orders of $100 or more, and $15 below that.
- **HST** of 13% applies to the items and the shipping.

## Tracking and cancelling orders

Your account page lists every order. Open one to see its items, address and progress:

| Status | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Pending | Received. You can still cancel it yourself. |
| Processing | Being picked and packed. |
| Shipped | On its way. |
| Delivered | Arrived. |
| Cancelled | Stopped before shipping; its items went back into stock. |

While an order is **Pending**, a **Cancel order** button appears on its page.

## Your account

Change your password from your account page; you will need the current one. If you enter the wrong
password five times in a row, sign-in pauses for up to fifteen minutes.
